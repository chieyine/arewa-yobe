# Draft user requirements specification

Status: derived from the supplied EOI and engineering specification. RAWYOD consultation, approval and formal UAT have not happened in this workspace.

Users are Civic Intelligence Focal Persons, Data/MEAL reviewers and administrators. Initial coverage is all 17 Yobe LGAs with 34 fictional focal accounts in the evaluation. The platform must support structured project/service observations, location and visit dates, optional photographs, review/clarification/verification, approval/rejection, traceable follow-up, scoped monitoring and exports. The working implementation maps to R01–R22 in `requirements-matrix.md`.

Key journeys are documented in `demo-walkthrough.md` and exposed in the in-app Help view. Approval does not publish a report or automatically confirm project progress. Follow-up visits stay linked to one item. Offline work is explicitly device-local until acknowledged by the server.

Pending client decisions: onboarding/provider delivery, final role scopes and administrator allowance to report, vocabulary/categories, language review, retention/safeguarding procedures, incident contact, hosting/region/budget, support duration, training schedule, acceptance criteria, and contractual ownership transfer. None are presented as client-approved.
